## The text that gets published

**[Date]. The thesis is withdrawn.**

Falsifier **[F#]** fired today: **[the reading, with its window and source]**,
against the line written on 14 November 2026 — **[the threshold]**.

Under the rule stated before the fact — one triggered falsifier downgrades
the verdict, two concurrent end it — the verdict *"priced for delivery"* no
longer stands. What replaces it depends on which line fired, and that was
also written in advance:

- **If F1 fired** (30-day average Intents volume below $75M/day): the demand
  claim is withdrawn. The settlement business shrank below the level the
  thesis required to be taken seriously, and no capture rate rescues a
  volume that is not there. I was wrong that the volume regime of
  August–November 2026 was a floor.
- **If F2 fired** (net take below 3 bps, or capture below 15%, on the
  60-day window): the capture claim is withdrawn. Volume without capture is
  someone else's business running over NEAR's rails, and the buyback engine
  the thesis priced does not exist at that take. I was wrong that the
  measured 5.13 bps was a rate rather than a moment.
- **If F3 fired** (HSP-029 not live on 30 June 2027): the scarcity claim is
  withdrawn in its dated form. The 1.6% leg of the threshold arithmetic was
  conditional on a governance process completing; it did not complete on
  the schedule the thesis allowed, and the 2.5% column is the honest one.
- **If F4 fired** (buyback-wallet outflow above 5% of the balance in
  30 days): the sink claim is withdrawn. The wallet was described — from a
  full-history census — as an accumulator that had released 0.5% of its
  lifetime inflows. A material outflow makes it a treasury, not a sink, and
  every absorption figure in the thesis falls with that reclassification.
- **If F5 fired** (four consecutive weeks of net ETF outflows, once a
  public endpoint exists to read it): the institutional-demand assumption
  is withdrawn to the extent it was ever load-bearing; the thesis said
  "cannot check" and meant it as a gap, not a comfort.
- **If F6 fired** (net-revenue run-rate below 2× its level four quarters
  earlier): the growth claim is withdrawn. 15.8× was the reading at
  submission; a stall to under 2× means the monetisation pillar stopped
  compounding, and with it the only path by which the threshold in §6 was
  ever going to be approached.

What does **not** get withdrawn: the measurements. The census, the
backtest, the threshold tracked through time, and the readings on this
board were computed from shipped data by a checker anyone can re-run; they
stand or fall on their own reproducibility, not on the verdict they were
marshalled for.
